Fleetsu is an Australian connected-fleet and GPS telematics platform offering real-time vehicle tracking, fuel management and fuel tax credit reporting, ATO-compliant automated logbooks, driver safety scoring, AI video dashcams, location alerts, asset tracking and service reminders for car, truck, trailer and plant fleets. Its fleetsu.com site is now branded Rand McNally ("Rand Platform", (c) 2025 Rand McNally) and its help center redirects to help.randmcnally.io. The Fleetsu Platform API, documented at api-docs.fleetsu.com, is a REST/JSON API over HTTPS using HTTP Basic authentication, covering assets, accounts, drivers, events, faults, fences, groups, logbooks, trips, reports and more.

**Areas:** Company · Fleet Management · Telematics · GPS Tracking · Connected Vehicles · Logistics · Driver Safety · Australia
